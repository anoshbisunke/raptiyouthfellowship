WHO WE ARE

A Fellowship
Rooted in Christ

Rapti Nagar Christian Youth Fellowship brings young people together to grow in the Word of God, encourage one another, worship, pray, and serve with love.

Our desire is to see every young person discover hope in Jesus Christ and become a light in their family, church, and community.
